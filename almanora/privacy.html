Privacy Policy

Almanora for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Last updated: 2026-11-01.

Your data

Almanora collects no personal data.

Network requests

To show a day, the app sends the chosen month and day to today.zenquotes.io. The request carries no account, no name and no device identifier. Like every web request, it shows your IP address to the ZenQuotes server.

Data on your device

The app stores the loaded days on the device, for up to 30 days. These files never leave the device.

No tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Wikipedia links

A tap on a Wikipedia link opens the Wikipedia page. The privacy policy of Wikipedia applies to that page.
