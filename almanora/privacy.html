Almanora [ Privacy ]

Almanora for iPhone, iPad and Mac. rev: 2026-11-26

--[ Your data ]

Almanora collects no personal data.

--[ Network requests ]

To show a day, the app sends the chosen month and day to today.zenquotes.io. The request carries no account, no name and no device identifier. Like every web request, it shows your IP address to the ZenQuotes server.

--[ Third parties ]

Almanora shares no personal data with third parties. ZenQuotes receives only the month and day. A tap on a Wikipedia link opens the Wikipedia page, and Wikipedia's privacy policy applies to that page.

--[ Data on your device ]

The app stores the loaded days on the device, for up to 30 days. These files never leave the device.

--[ No tracking ]

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

--[ Delete your data ]

Almanora collects no personal data, so I hold nothing that could be deleted. For any question or request about your data, write to sibe1024[at]gmx{dot}net.

--[ Changes ]

Changes to this policy appear on this page, with a new rev date.
