Common questions

Where do the entries come from?

Almanora loads each day from today.zenquotes.io. The entries and links come from Wikipedia.

How do I see another day?

Tap a day in the week strip, or swipe sideways. The calendar button picks any month and day. On the Mac, the Go menu has Previous Day, Next Day and Today.

Why does the app say "Offline"?

The network failed, so the app shows the saved copy of the day. Tap Retry when the network is back. On the Mac, Reload Day (Command-R) does the same.

How do I hide births or deaths?

Open the settings and switch off Show births or Show deaths. On iPhone and iPad, tap the gear button. On the Mac, choose Almanora > Settings.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. The app keeps loaded days for up to 30 days and has no account.
