Privacy Policy

ISSeeYou for Mac, iPhone and Apple TV. Last updated: 2026-11-01.

Your data

ISSeeYou collects no personal data. It never asks for your location.

Network requests

The app loads the position of the International Space Station from api.wheretheiss.at. To find the country below the station, it sends the station's coordinates to the same server. It never sends your own position.

The requests carry no account, no name and no device identifier. Like every web request, they show your IP address to the wheretheiss.at server.

Map

The map comes from Apple Maps. Apple's privacy policy applies to the map data.

Data on your device

The app stores your settings on the device: appearance, units and refresh interval. These settings never leave the device.

No account, no tracking

ISSeeYou has no account and no login. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
