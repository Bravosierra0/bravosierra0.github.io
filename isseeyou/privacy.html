ISSeeYou [ Privacy ]

ISSeeYou for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. rev: 2026-11-26

--[ Your data ]

ISSeeYou collects no personal data and never asks for your location. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

--[ Network requests ]

The app loads the station's position from api.wheretheiss.at. To find the country below the station, it sends the station's coordinates to the same server. It never sends your position.

The requests carry no account, name or device identifier. Like any web request, they show your IP address to that server.

--[ Third parties ]

No personal data goes to third parties. The map comes from Apple Maps, and Apple's privacy policy applies to the map data.

--[ Data on your device ]

Your settings (appearance, units, refresh interval) stay on the device. Deleting the app removes them.

--[ Delete your data ]

ISSeeYou collects no personal data, so I hold nothing to delete. For questions or requests about your data, write to sibe1024[at]gmx{dot}net.

Changes to this policy appear on this page, with a new rev date.
