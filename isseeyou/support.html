Common questions

Where does the position come from?

ISSeeYou loads the position of the International Space Station from wheretheiss.at.

Where is the app on my Mac?

ISSeeYou runs in the menu bar, not in the Dock. Click its icon in the menu bar to open it.

How often does the position update?

Open the settings and choose a value under Refresh every. The choices go from 5 seconds to 15 minutes.

How do I switch between kilometers and miles?

Open the settings and choose a value under Units to display.

Why does the map not move?

Check that Auto Refresh is on. The app also needs a network connection to load the position.
