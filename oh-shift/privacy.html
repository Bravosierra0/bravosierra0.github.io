Privacy Policy

Oh Shift! for iPhone and iPad. Last updated: 2026-11-01.

Your data

Oh Shift! collects no personal data. It has no server and sends nothing over the network.

The shift types and departments you create stay on your device.

Calendar access

Oh Shift! saves each shift as an event in its own calendar, named "Oh Shift!". To do this, it asks for full access to your calendars.

The app reads and writes events only in the "Oh Shift!" calendar. It does not read your other calendars.

iCloud

If you use iCloud Calendar, Oh Shift! creates its calendar in your iCloud account. Apple then syncs your shifts like any other calendar event. Otherwise the calendar stays on your device.

Widget

The Home Screen widget reads a short list of your next shifts. The app stores this list in a container on your device that only the app and its widget can open.

No account, no tracking

Oh Shift! has no account and no login. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Deleting your data

To remove a shift, delete it in the app. This also removes the event from your calendar.

Deleting the app removes your shift types and departments. The "Oh Shift!" calendar and its events stay in the Calendar app. To remove them, delete the "Oh Shift!" calendar in the Calendar app.
