Oh Shift! [ Privacy ]

Oh Shift! for iPhone and iPad. rev: 20261126

--[ Your data ]

Oh Shift! collects no personal data. It has no server, sends nothing over the network, and has no account, analytics, ads or tracking.

Your shift types and departments stay on your device.

--[ Calendar access ]

Oh Shift! saves each shift as an event in its own calendar, named "Oh Shift!". For this it asks for full calendar access. It reads and writes events only in that calendar.

--[ iCloud ]

If you use iCloud Calendar, the "Oh Shift!" calendar lives in your iCloud account, and Apple syncs it like any other calendar. Apple's privacy policy applies.

--[ Third parties ]

No personal data goes to third parties.

--[ Data on your device ]

The Home Screen widget reads your next shifts from storage that only the app and its widget can open.

Delete a shift in the app to remove it and its calendar event. Deleting the app removes your shift types and departments. The "Oh Shift!" calendar stays in the Calendar app until you delete it there.

--[ Delete your data ]

Oh Shift! collects no personal data, so I hold nothing to delete. For questions or requests about your data, write to sibe1024[at]gmx{dot}net.

--[ Changes ]

Changes to this policy appear on this page, with a new rev date.
