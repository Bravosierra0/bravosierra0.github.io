Oh Shift! [ Privacy ]

Oh Shift! for iPhone and iPad. rev: 2026-11-26

--[ Your data ]

Oh Shift! collects no personal data. It has no server and sends nothing over the network.

The shift types and departments you create stay on your device.

--[ Calendar access ]

Oh Shift! saves each shift as an event in its own calendar, named "Oh Shift!". To do this, it asks for full access to your calendars.

The app reads and writes events only in the "Oh Shift!" calendar. It does not read your other calendars.

--[ iCloud ]

If you use iCloud Calendar, Oh Shift! creates its calendar in your iCloud account. Apple then syncs your shifts like any other calendar event, and Apple's privacy policy applies. Otherwise the calendar stays on your device.

--[ Third parties ]

Oh Shift! shares no personal data with third parties. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

--[ Data on your device ]

The Home Screen widget reads a short list of your next shifts from a container on your device that only the app and its widget can open.

To remove a shift, delete it in the app. This also removes the event from your calendar. Deleting the app removes your shift types and departments. The "Oh Shift!" calendar stays in the Calendar app until you delete it there.

--[ Delete your data ]

Oh Shift! collects no personal data, so I hold nothing that could be deleted. For any question or request about your data, write to sibe1024[at]gmx{dot}net.

--[ Changes ]

Changes to this policy appear on this page, with a new rev date.
