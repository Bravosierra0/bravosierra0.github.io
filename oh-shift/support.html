Common questions

Where are my shifts stored?

In the Calendar app, in a calendar named "Oh Shift!". If you use iCloud Calendar, Apple syncs it like your other calendars.

Why does the app ask for calendar access?

Oh Shift! saves each shift as a calendar event. It reads and writes events only in its own "Oh Shift!" calendar.

I denied calendar access. How do I turn it on?

Open the Settings app, tap Apps, then Oh Shift!, then Calendars. Choose Full Access.

How do I get a reminder before a shift?

Open the shift and pick a reminder. The Calendar app shows the alert, like for any other event.

I deleted the app. Where are my shifts?

They are still in the "Oh Shift!" calendar in the Calendar app. To remove them, delete that calendar in the Calendar app.
