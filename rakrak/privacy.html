Rak Rak [ Privacy ]

Rak Rak for iPhone and iPad. rev: 20261126

--[ Your data ]

Rak Rak collects no personal data. It has no server, no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

The occasions you add, with any note or photo, stay on your device.

--[ iCloud sync ]

If you use iCloud, Rak Rak syncs your occasions through your own iCloud account (CloudKit). Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

--[ Permissions ]

Notifications: only after you tap Enable Notifications.

Photos: you pick a photo in the system photo picker, and Rak Rak receives only that photo.

--[ Third parties ]

No personal data goes to third parties. Rak Rak uses no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

--[ Data on your device ]

Delete an occasion in the app to remove it. If iCloud sync is on, this also removes it from iCloud.

Deleting the app removes the data from this device only. To remove the iCloud copy too, delete your occasions and categories in the app first.

--[ Delete your data ]

Rak Rak collects no personal data, so I hold nothing to delete. For questions or requests about your data, write to sibe1024[at]gmx{dot}net.

--[ Changes ]

Changes to this policy appear on this page, with a new rev date.
