Rak Rak [ Privacy ]

Rak Rak for iPhone and iPad. rev: 2026-11-26

--[ Your data ]

Rak Rak collects no personal data. It has no server, so nothing you enter reaches me.

The app stores the occasions you add on your device: each date, its category, and any note or photo you attach.

--[ iCloud sync ]

If you sign in to iCloud, Rak Rak syncs this data through your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

--[ Third parties ]

Rak Rak shares no personal data with third parties. It uses no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

--[ Permissions ]

Rak Rak asks for one permission: Notifications, when you tap Enable Notifications.

To attach a photo, you pick it in the system photo picker. Rak Rak only receives that photo.

--[ Data on your device ]

To remove an occasion, delete it in the app. If iCloud sync is on, this also removes it from your iCloud account.

Deleting the app removes the data from your device only. If iCloud sync is on, the copy in iCloud stays. To remove it, delete your occasions and categories in the app, then delete the app.

--[ Delete your data ]

Rak Rak collects no personal data, so I hold nothing that could be deleted. For any question or request about your data, write to sibe1024[at]gmx{dot}net.

--[ Changes ]

Changes to this policy appear on this page, with a new rev date.
