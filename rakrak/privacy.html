Privacy Policy

Rak Rak for iPhone and iPad. Last updated: 2026-09-26.

Your data

Rak Rak stores the occasions you add: each date, its category, and any note or photo you attach. Rak Rak saves this data on your device.

iCloud sync

If you sign in to iCloud, Rak Rak syncs this data through your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. It does not pass through any Rak Rak server, because there is none.

No account, no tracking

Rak Rak has no account and no login. It uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK.

Permissions

Rak Rak asks for one permission: Notifications, when you tap Enable Notifications.

To attach a photo, you pick it in the system photo picker. Rak Rak only receives that photo.

Deleting your data

To remove an occasion, delete it in the app. If iCloud sync is on, this also removes it from your iCloud account.

Deleting the app removes the data from your device only. If iCloud sync is on, the copy in iCloud stays. To remove it, delete your occasions and categories in the app, then delete the app.
