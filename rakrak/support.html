Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you sign in to iCloud, Rak Rak also syncs it through your own iCloud account.

How do I show an occasion on the Home Screen widget?

Swipe right on the occasion to pin it, or turn on Pinned in Edit. Then add the Rak Rak widget to your Home Screen.

How do I change the app's language?

Settings > Language. Rak Rak supports English, German, and Thai.

How do I get a reminder for an occasion?

Open the occasion, tap Edit, turn on Same Day, and pick a Time. Second alert adds a reminder 1 to 30 days before.

How do I remove an occasion and its data?

Open the occasion and tap Delete. If iCloud sync is on, this also removes it from iCloud.
